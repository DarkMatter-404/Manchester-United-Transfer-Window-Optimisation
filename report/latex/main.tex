{\centering\bfseries Abstract\par}
\vspace{0.18cm}

This report models Manchester United's summer 2025 transfer window
as a binary integer linear program. Historical match, transfer and
valuation records through 31 May 2025, together with disclosed wage
and profile approximations, inform purchases, sales, squad selection
and role assignments in a 3-4-2-1 formation. The model maximises a
role-based quality proxy subject to transfer and wage allowances,
positional cover, registration rules and transfer limits. Under the
base assumptions, Gurobi finds an optimal plan with six purchases
and three sales, net spending of \pounds 198.69m against a
\pounds 250m allowance, and annual wages of \pounds 147.94m against
a \pounds 148m allowance. The tested scenarios show greater
sensitivity to wages and arrival limits than to the transfer
allowance. Later player outcomes are presented descriptively
and do not establish the plan's sporting effectiveness.
\par
