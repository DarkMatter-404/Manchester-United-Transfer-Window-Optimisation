\section*{Statement on assistance}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Statement on assistance}

Two AI tools were used in this project. Claude (developed by Anthropic, used through Claude Code) assisted, under the team's direction, with writing and testing the Python code, preparing the data tables and the figure, checking the references against their publication records, and drafting and revising this report. ChatGPT (developed by OpenAI, used through Codex) was used by team members to review drafts of the report and to suggest revisions. The team decided the modelling rules and parameter values, which are recorded with dates in the project's decision log, chose which suggested revisions to adopt, and is responsible for the analysis and the submitted report.
