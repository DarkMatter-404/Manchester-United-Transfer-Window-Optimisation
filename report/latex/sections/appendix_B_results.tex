A \emph{direct alternative} keeps the other five purchases and the three sales fixed, rules out the earlier options for that need and requires the replacement to start in the same role, while the rest of the line-up may change (Table~\ref{tab:directfull}). Each was evaluated with the other five base purchases in place, so combining replacements from different rows would need another feasibility check. The deal labels follow written rules. A deal is \emph{likely} if the player is leaving without a fee, played under half of the club's league minutes or plays for a club that finished 15th or lower; \emph{doubtful} if the player is at a direct rival or a wealthy club, is a regular starter at a top-four club, would cost \pounds 75m or more, or was reported before the cutoff to have agreed a move elsewhere; and \emph{possible} otherwise. The labels are team judgement written as rules, not probabilities. Martín Zubimendi (CM, plan B) and Joan García (GK, plan D) were reported before the cutoff to have agreed moves that were not announced by then (Table~\ref{tab:handlists}); under the announcement rule of Section~\ref{sec:data} they stay available, so both are conditional on those agreements not being binding.
